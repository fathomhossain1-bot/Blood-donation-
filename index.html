How blood donation works · কীভাবে কাজ করে

Frequently asked questions · সচরাচর প্রশ্ন


Who can donate blood?

Generally healthy adults aged 18–60, weighing at least 50 kg. A doctor or blood bank makes the final decision.

How often can I donate? / কতদিন পর পর রক্ত দেওয়া যায়?

Men can usually donate every 3–4 months; women every 4 months. Please follow the advice of your blood bank.

Is my phone number public?

No. Your number is never shown on this website. It is only shared with verified blood seekers, and only if you gave consent.

Does BloodBond guarantee a donor?

No. We help connect people, but availability and medical eligibility cannot be guaranteed.

Do I get paid?

No. BloodBond is for voluntary donation only. Never pay anyone for blood.

Privacy Policy

We collect only what is needed to connect donors and blood seekers: name, phone, blood group, location and availability.

Your details are never published. They are shared with verified blood seekers only if you tick the consent box.

Submitted forms are delivered to our private volunteer team. You may ask us to delete your data at any time by contacting us.

Terms of Use

BloodBond is a connection service for voluntary donors. We do not guarantee availability, quality, or medical suitability of any donation.

Buying or selling blood is not allowed. Always verify donors and patients through a hospital or licensed blood bank.

Provide accurate information. Misuse may result in removal.
